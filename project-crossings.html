The tension

A fixed timeline makes strategy difficult to sense.

Historical facts alone could not convey the spatial judgement and changing conditions that made each crossing consequential.

Design move

Let movement reveal the strategy.

Routes, terrain, and changing scale make tactical choices visible as visitors move through the narrative rather than simply read about it.

What I made

A map that can be explored like a story.

A bilingual digital narrative that turns the Chishui River campaign into a paced, interactive field of decisions.
