The tension

Support often arrives only after someone has to ask for it.

Young adults living alone can experience vulnerability without a clear or comfortable threshold for reaching out.

Research signal

Weak ties matter before a crisis.

Everyday contact, shared space, and light-touch prompts can make connection feel voluntary rather than prescribed.

Design move

Make participation visible, gentle, and reciprocal.

The service joins digital prompts, shared cultivation, spatial touchpoints, and layered support into a community-care system.
