Future 03B / Urban systems study

Future
Streets.

A speculative study of how delivery robots can enter Singapore HDB neighbourhoods without interrupting shared everyday life.

Role
Research and interaction design
Format
Speculative web study
Focus
Coexistence, culture, systems
Enter live site ↗

The tension

Automation enters a street that already has a rhythm.

Shared HDB spaces carry informal social rules, varied pedestrian speeds, and everyday uses that cannot be treated as an empty delivery route.

Research signal

Coexistence depends on more than technical clearance.

Observation and future research bring cultural expectation, spatial etiquette, and moments of friction into the design question.

Design move

Speculate from the lived street outward.

A future-facing interactive study that treats robots as one participant in a shared public system, not the system's centre.
