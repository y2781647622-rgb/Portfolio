Kitchen.

A dark fantasy management game built around the rhythm of hunting, cooking, service, and survival.

Role
Independent designer
Format
Browser game
Focus

The tension

Build atmosphere through actions, not only artwork.

The world needed to feel alive while still giving players a clear rhythm to learn and return to.

Design move

Make the loop carry the mood.

Hunting, cooking, resource management, and service are designed as connected choices rather than isolated game mechanics.

What I made

An interactive world with its own logic.

A browser game that joins visual direction, gameplay pacing, and interface decisions into one playable system.
